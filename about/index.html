ABOUT

Learning that feels close to home.

ChitraMitra is a free, India-first printable learning platform for children ages 3–8. V1 focuses on Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam, with culturally relevant topics and simple activities.

We use structured learning objects so one concept can be reused across languages, ages and printable formats.
